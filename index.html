Elevated Flavors.

Located in Rapid City at the Western entrance to the Black Hills, the Blue Heron pairs traditional French technique with the agricultural heritage and flavors of the Northern Great Plains.

We are locally owned & operated, with a scratch kitchen committed to creating wonderful seasonal dishes, and a service staff committed to a casual and comprehensive experience.

Do yourself a flavor and plan your dinner visit today.
